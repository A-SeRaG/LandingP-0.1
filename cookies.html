Cookie Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This site does not set cookies. Here is what it does store in your browser.

Stored on your device

  • Theme preference (cmind-theme in local storage): remembers whether you chose the light or dark theme.
  • Offline cache: a service worker saves copies of the site's own pages and files so it loads faster and works offline.

Third parties

  • Google Fonts loads fonts from Google servers when a page opens.
  • Formspree is contacted only when you submit the demo request form.

No tracking

We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, so there is no cookie banner to accept.

Your choices

You can clear local storage and cached files at any time in your browser settings. The site still works without them.
