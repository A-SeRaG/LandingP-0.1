Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy covers the cmind.tech marketing website. CMind is based in Cairo, Egypt. You can reach us at hello@cmind.tech.

What we collect

Only what you type into the demo request form: school name, contact person, email address, phone number (optional), number of students (optional), the track you are interested in, and your message.

How we use it

We use it to reply to your request and discuss a possible partnership. We do not sell it and we do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Formspree receives the form submission and forwards it to our team by email.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts used on this site, so your browser contacts Google servers when a page loads.
  • Our hosting provider receives standard technical request data (such as IP address) to deliver the site.

What we do not do

This site has no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. It is written for school staff and decision makers, and we do not knowingly collect information from children through it.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you sent us, in line with Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020). Email hello@cmind.tech and we will respond.

The CMind learning platform

When the CMind learning platform launches for schools, a separate notice will explain how student data is handled, including parental consent and how long data is kept.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
